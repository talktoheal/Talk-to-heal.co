Our Story

Talk To Heal began with a simple, stubborn belief: nobody should have to sit with a heavy heart completely alone. In a world that moves fast and listens rarely, we wanted to build a gentler pause — a place where your feelings don't need explaining, your privacy is never in question, and someone is always willing to simply listen.

FAQs

Good to know

Is Talk To Heal therapy or counselling?

No. We're an emotional support and listening service, not a medical or therapy platform. We don't diagnose or treat anything — we simply listen, without judgment.

Do I have to share my name?

Never. You can chat or call completely anonymously, start to finish.

What happens to what I share?

Every conversation is confidential. Nothing is shared, saved to a public profile, or traced back to you.

What if I'm in a crisis or emergency?

Talk To Heal is not an emergency service. If you're in immediate danger or having thoughts of self-harm, please call Tele MANAS at 14416 (India's free, 24×7 government mental health helpline) or dial 112 right away.

How much does a session cost?

Chat sessions start at ₹99 and calls at ₹199 for 30 minutes (limited-time discounted pricing). See Pricing above for full details.

Is this only for adults?

Yes, Talk To Heal is intended for users aged 18 and above.

If you're in crisis right now

Talk To Heal offers emotional support, not emergency care. If you're in immediate danger or having thoughts of suicide, please call Tele MANAS at 14416 (free, 24×7) or dial 112 immediately. If you're under 18, please also reach out to a trusted adult.

Help & Support

Need a hand using the site?

How do I start chatting with a listener?

Tap any "Start Chat" button, or the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of the screen.

How do I start a call instead?

Tap any "Call Now" button — it will dial our number directly from your phone.

Will I get a reply to my anonymous message?

That box is a private space to let your thoughts out. For an actual back-and-forth conversation, please start a chat or call with a listener directly.

Something on the site isn't working — what do I do?

Try refreshing the page and checking your internet connection first. If it still doesn't work, reach us directly on WhatsApp or call — we'll sort it out quickly.

Does this work on my phone?

Yes, the whole site works smoothly on both mobile and desktop.

My question isn't answered here — who do I contact?

Reach us on WhatsApp, call, or email — see the Contact section just below, and we'll get back to you quickly.
